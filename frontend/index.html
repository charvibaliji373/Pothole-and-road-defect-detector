THE PROBLEM STATEMENT & WHY IT MATTERS

Why Pothole Detection Demands Modern AI

⚠️

The Problem with Traditional Inspection

  • Manual Walking Surveys: Municipal inspectors walk on active, high-speed roadways with tape measures, creating serious safety hazards.
  • Weeks of Delay: By the time an inspection report is filed on paper, water seepage expands a 20cm crack into a dangerous 1-meter sinkhole.
  • Severe Accidents & Costs: Potholes cause wheel rim blowouts, suspension destruction, motorcycle crashes, and millions in municipal liability claims.
  • No Material Forecasting: Road crews arrive with either too much or too little asphalt, wasting municipal taxpayers' money.
🛡️

The RoadVision AI Solution ✨

  • 150ms Automated Vision: Analyzes photos and video streams from dashcams, smartphones, and survey vehicles instantly.
  • ASTM D6433 Pavement Index (PCI): Automatically scores road condition from 0 (Failed) to 100 (Good), eliminating guesswork.
  • Exact Asphalt Mass Calculation: Computes required hot-mix asphalt (HMA) volume in metric tons using 2,400 kg/m³ density models.
  • End-to-End Civic Dispatch: Citizens report hazards via phone with auto-GPS; municipalities manage repair work orders on a Kanban board.

Backbone & Feature Fusion

  • Base Architecture: Ultralytics YOLOv8n (Nano) with modified C2f bottleneck blocks.
  • Feature Pyramid Network (FPN + PAN): Bi-directional multiscale feature aggregation for both micro-fissures and wide road cavities.
  • Anchor-Free Head: Directly regresses center offsets and bounding box dimensions, reducing hyperparameter sensitivity.
  • Training Hyperparameters: 30 Epochs, Batch Size 2/4, Image Size 416x416, AdamW optimizer.

Civil Engineering Innovation

  • ASTM D6433 Integration: Translates visual 2D pixel bounding boxes into standard pavement distress deduct points.
  • Asphalt Tonnage Engine: Computes required hot-mix asphalt (HMA) volume using geometric depth models and standard 2,400 kg/m³ density.
  • Dual Currency Estimator: Calculates localized civil repair budgets in both USD ($) and INR (₹).
  • Multi-Frame Tracking: IoU centroid matching prevents duplicate counting across continuous dashcam video routes.
